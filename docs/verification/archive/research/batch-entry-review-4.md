## Notes

- The Doteli entries (i=413-419) are all very similar, sharing most place names. This is expected since they are dialects of the same language spoken in adjacent districts.
- The Cape Verdean Creole entries (i=181, 182, 189) have cross-contamination between Barlavento and Sotavento island groups. This may reflect historical population movement or incomplete data.
- Kenaboi (i=133) is an extinct language, but the place names are well-documented in the region where it was spoken.
- South Halmahera (i=180) is the most problematic entry - it's not a single language and should probably be split into constituent languages or removed.
