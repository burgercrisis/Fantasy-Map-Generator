## Methodology

For each entry in the specified range, the corresponding Wikipedia article was fetched and reviewed. Place names in the `b` field were checked for:
1. **Regional correctness** — names belong to the language's actual geographic region
2. **Type appropriateness** — names are settlements/geographic features, not administrative unit names (states, provinces, regions) or language/ethnicity names used as place names
3. **Plausibility** — names are real, verifiable places
