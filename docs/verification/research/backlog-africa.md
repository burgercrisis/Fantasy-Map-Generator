### Notes on individual entries

**1338 Bade** — Bade is a West Chadic language of Yobe and Jigawa States, Nigeria (Wikipedia *Bade
language*; ISO `bde`). Seeds are the Bade LGA postal-code village list plus the Jakusko LGA villages
(also Bade-speaking per the Yobe State language-by-LGA table) plus the Bade dialect towns named on
Omniglot. `Gashua` is the Bade LGA headquarters; `Katanga(n)a`, `Gorgoram`, `Dagona`, `Tagali` and
`Madamuwa` are the Southern/Western Bade towns named by Omniglot.

**265 Adeni Arabic** — Wikipedia redirects *Adeni Arabic* to *Taʽizzi-Adeni Arabic* (ISO `acq`,
southern Yemeni Arabic, native to Taiz and Aden governorates). Seeds are the cities of those two
governorates, the eight Aden districts, the Ta'izz district seats named in the Ta'izz Governorate
article, and the three contiguous Ibb/Abyan/Lahij towns where the dialect also has speakers.

**266 Aleppine Arabic** — the northern Syrian variety. Seeds are district seats of Aleppo, Idlib,
Latakia, Tartus, Hama, Raqqa, Homs-west and Deir ez-Zor, i.e. the Aleppine dialect area. Not
Damascus, Daraa, Suwayda or al-Hasakah, which are outside the dialect area.

**1721 Duhwa** — Wikipedia/Glottolog place Duhwa (also Karfa, Kerifa, Nzuhwi, ISO `kbz`) in Nasarawa
State, Nigeria, in the Ron (A.4) group. Seeds are the named towns and district seats of Nasarawa State
from the state article. Marked **WAITING** (23 < 25): no Duhwa-specific village gazetteer was found,
so these are the towns of the language's own state rather than documented Duhwa villages.

**202541 Nagpuri** — Nagpuri is the Bihari language of the Chotanagpur plateau; the Hazaribagh district
article lists it as a mother tongue (3.62% "Others incl. Nagpuri"). Seeds are the cities, towns and
villages of Ranchi, Khunti, Hazaribagh, Bokaro, Latehar, Gumla, Lohardaga and Simdega districts.

**202542 Naiki** — Central Dravidian, spoken in south-western Odisha and adjacent Chhattisgarh. Only
11 seeds: the intent was a list that is **not** a subset of Sambalpuri (202619), because the two
languages share most of their area and the integrity gate flags identical/subset seed lists. Marked
**WAITING** honestly rather than padded with overlapping names.

**202619 Sambalpuri** — Eastern Indo-Aryan, western Odisha. Seeds are census towns, municipalities and
NACs of the Sambalpuri-speaking districts per the Census of India derived
`citypopulation.de/en/india/odisha` table and the Odisha urban-local-bodies list.

## 3. Findings that belong in another file (reported, not touched)

1. **`public/modules/namebases-africa.js` is massively misfiled.** Of the 171 zero-seed entries it
   contained at the start of this task, only about 8 are African. The file also holds (as zero-seed
   entries) roughly 100 **South Asian** languages (Doteli group, Tamang, Thangmi, Magar, Newar,
   Tamang, Sherpa, Sadri, Dogri, Pahari-Pothwari, all the Zhuang/Bonan/Na Nang entries, all the
   Santa Mongolian entries, the Mizo/Naga/Mmik entries), about **28 Australian Aboriginal** languages,
   about 12 **Chinese** (Hmong, Hmu, She, Northern Min, Shanghainese, Sanqiao, Pingtang), plus
   **Montserrat and Limonese Creole**. The backup confirms the whole block was misfiled, not just
   the empty part. Moving entries between continent files is outside my file ownership.
   There is a `tools/namebase-tools/check-continent-assignments.js` that may already report this.

2. **`public/config/language-mixer-map.js` — two rows point at the wrong language.** Both are in
   files I do not own; flagged, not touched.
   - `northern-mindoro` → **202839**. Index 202839 is **"Northern Min"**, a Chinese Min language.
     There is **no "Northern Mindoro" entry in any namebase file** — the row resolves to a language
     from the wrong continent. (This is the second independent confirmation of the northAmerica and
     asia agents' "wrong target row" finding.)
   - `ful` → **202766**, and 202766 is **"Wagiman"** (an Australian Aboriginal language). `ful` is
     Fula, a real West African language. Fula already exists as entry **1854** in this file.
     Recommend repointing `ful` at 1854 rather than creating anything new.

3. **Duplicated `i` values in the backlog are benign** but worth recording: the backlog's 70 rows map
   to fewer distinct entries because one entry can carry several ISO codes. `1338` = `bade-chadic` +
   `bade-language`; `200144` = `pan-chadic` + `pan`; `202625` = `santa-suonanba` +
   `x-santa-suonanba`; `202766` = `waq` + `ful`; `202839` = `northern-min` + `northern-mindoro`.
   **`namebases-africa.js` itself has 863 entries and zero duplicate `i`** (checked).

4. **The seed separator is a comma, not a pipe.** `tools/namebase-tools/verify-namebase-integrity.js:263`
   splits on `","`. A first pass that used `|` produced `E008 ... has only 1 seed(s)` for every entry
   touched. Anyone scripting writes against these files must join with `,`. The canonical writer is
   `window.africaNameBases = ` + `JSON.stringify(entries, null, 2)` + `;\n`.
