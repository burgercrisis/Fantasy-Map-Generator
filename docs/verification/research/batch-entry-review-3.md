### ENTRY: Orang Pulo (i=127) | STATUS: ISSUES | ISSUES: Misidentified place names

**Language region:** Thousand Islands (Kepulauan Seribu), Jakarta Bay, Indonesia

**Place names in namebase:**
- Panggang, Pramuka, Kelapa, Harapan, Tidung, Untung Jawa, Pari, Sebira, Kotok, Ayer, Bidadari, Bokor, Bira, Bulat, Bundar, Burung, Cipir, Damar, Dapur, Cina, Dua, Genteng, Gosong, Gudus, Gundul, Hantu, Layang, Melintang, Melinjo, Nyamuk, Onrust, Pantara, Pelangi, Petir, Pulau H, Rambut, Sepa, Seraya, Sepang, Tanjung, Tidung Kecil, Untung Jawa Kecil, Wanara

**ISSUES:**
These names are all small islands in Jakarta Bay (Kepulauan Seribu / Thousand Islands). While Orang Pulo (also called Orang Laut or "Sea Gypsies") do live in Jakarta Bay, these are island names, not settlement/town names. The Orang Pulo are a maritime people, not specifically tied to these islands as "place names" in the traditional sense.

However, this is a borderline case - the Orang Pulo do inhabit these islands, so the names are geographically correct for the group's location. The issue is that these are tiny uninhabited or minimally inhabited islets rather than proper settlements. For a fantasy map generator, these would render as very small island labels rather than town names.

**Recommendation:** Consider whether these should be replaced with actual Orang Pulo settlement names (e.g., Pulau Tidung, Pulau Pramuka, Pulau Kelapa are the main inhabited ones) or kept as-is if the intent is to show the Thousand Islands geography.
