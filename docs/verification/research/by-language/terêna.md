## Phonology Check

- Source: SAPHON (Berkeley), Wikipedia Terêna phonology section, Silva 2009 (UFMS)
- Key patterns:
  - Consonants: p, t, k, ʔ, s, ʃ, h, hʲ, m, n, (ɲ), j, w, ɾ, l
  - Vowels: i, e, a, o, u (with nasalized allophones ĩ, ẽ, ã, õ, ũ and long iː, eː, aː, oː, uː)
  - Syllable structure: (C)V(V) — no consonant clusters in native words
  - Stress: trochaic (left-headed binary feet)
  - Glottal stop /ʔ/ is phonemic
  - Post-alveolar fricative /ʃ/ exists
- Names flagged: None — all names are Portuguese-rendered forms of official Terena community names used by the Terena people themselves in government documents (CIMI, Funai). The underlying Terena toponyms follow Terena phonotactics.

## Notes

- All 36 original names were Portuguese-language municipality/city names in Mato Grosso do Sul — administrative units, not Terêna settlements
- All 34 replacement names individually verified with per-name sources (see Names Added table)
- The Terêna people live in ~34+ villages across 9+ indigenous lands (TIs) in Mato Grosso do Sul, plus urban communities in Campo Grande and Sidrolândia
- Most village names are Portuguese translations of Terêna concepts (e.g., Água Branca = Hopunó'evoti Úne, Lagoinha = Kali Lâvona) — these are the official names used by the communities
- Population: ~15,000-18,000 people (second largest indigenous group in MS)
