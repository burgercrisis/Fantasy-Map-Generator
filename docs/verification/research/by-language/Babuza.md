## Research Findings
From Wikipedia Babuza language:
- Extinct Formosan language (2000s)
- Spoken along much of the western coast of Taiwan
- Two dialects: Poavosa and Taokas
- Related to or descended from Favorlang

From Wikipedia Babuza people:
- "living primarily in Changhua County and around the western part of Taiwan's Central Basin"
- "between the Dadu (Ta-tu) River & the Zhuoshui River in what is now parts of southeastern Taichung & northwestern Changhua County"

From Wikipedia Kingdom of Middag:
- Supra-tribal alliance of Papora, Babuza, Pazeh, and Hoanya
- Ruled as many as 27 villages in western Taichung and northern Changhua
- Capital: Middag (modern Dadu)
- Other towns: Sada, Beodor, Deredonesel, Goema

From CloudBridgeTaiwan:
- "The Babuza traditionally made their home between the Dadu (Ta-tu) River & the Zhuoshui River"
- "The Kingdom of Dadu was established in the central-western plains of Taiwan in the 17th century by the Babuza People"

From Verblix:
- "It is spoken in West central coast and inland, Tatu and Choshui rivers and beyond"
