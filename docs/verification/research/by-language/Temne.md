## Research Log

1. **Language range established.** Joshua Project (citing Ethnologue 2016) for Themne [tem]: "Northern province: Port Loko, Tonkolili, and Bombali districts; west of Sewa river to Little Scarcie; Southern province: northwest Moyamba district; Western Area: Rural district." (https://joshuaproject.net/people_groups/15333/SL). Encyclopedia.com/everyculture (Temne — Orientation) adds Karene and Kambia and gives the bounding box 8°20′–9°20′ N, and states Temne are bounded "on the north by the Susu, Limba, and Loko; on the east by the Kuranko and Kono." This gives a hard test for each name.
2. **District-level ethnic dominance checked** on Wikipedia for Tonkolili ("Temne people make up the overwhelming majority"), Port Loko ("Temne people form by far the largest ethnic group"), Karene ("The Temne people is the predominant majority ethnic group"), Bombali ("Temne and Limba form the largest ethnic groups"), Kambia ("Temne, Susu, Limba, Fula, Mandingo"; "largest population of ethnic Susu in Sierra Leone"), Falaba ("Kuranko, Yalunka, Fula and Mandingo"; "largest population of ethnic Yalunka").
3. **Chiefdom-level filtering.** Because Bombali/Karene/Kambia are ethnically mixed, each surviving name was checked against its chiefdom. Chiefdoms carrying "Limba" in the name (Sella Limba → Kamakwie, Safroko Limba → Binkolo, Tonko Limba → Madina) and Biriwa (Limba) were treated as non-Temne. Susu chiefdoms Bramaia (Kukuna) and Samu (Kychom, Kassiri, Yeliboya) likewise removed. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombali_District, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karene_District, https://cdm21069.contentdm.oclc.org/digital/api/collection/ppl1/id/406818/download (Kambia chiefdom/HQ table).
4. **Chiefdom HQs confirmed as real settlements** via the Bombali District article's chiefdom table: Kamaranka, Kalangba, Batkanu, Hunduwa, Masongbon, Mapaki, Kamalo, Mateboi. Batkanu independently confirmed (village, Libeisaygahun Chiefdom, Karene District, 09°05′N 12°25′W) at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batkanu and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libeisaygahun_Chiefdom. Mateboi confirmed as seat of Sanda Tenraren Chiefdom (http://wikimapia.org/34013864/Mateboi).
5. **Port Loko towns confirmed** from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Loko_District: "The other major towns in the district include Masiaka, Rokupr, Pepel, Lungi and Gbinti." Capital Port Loko, largest city Lunsar.
6. **Tonkolili towns confirmed** from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonkolili_District: "capital and largest city is Magburaka. The other major towns include Masingbi, Yele, Mile 91, Bumbuna, Yonibana, Matotoka..."
7. **Temne etymologies cross-checked** on the Sierra Leone Web "Village Names" page (https://www.sierra-leone.org/villagenames.html):
   - **Lunsar** < Temne *Ro-Sar* "place where the stones are" (Marampa Chiefdom, Port Loko) — confirms a Temne-language settlement name.
   - **Petifu** (Port Loko) — hybrid Temne + Susu: Temne *pet* "place outside the bush" + Susu *fu* "new"; explicitly a mixed Fullah/Susu/Temne town → retained.
   - **Katema** (Mambolo Chiefdom, Kambia) — Temne *ka-tem-ma* "place of the bush fowl"; confirms Temne speech inside Mambolo Chiefdom → **Mambolo retained**.
   - **Rokoray** (Samu Chiefdom) — "Notwithstanding the dominance of the Temne language among the village inhabitants, the Soso cultural influence remains manifest"; noted, but the Samu wharf towns actually in the list (Kychom, Kassiri, Yeliboya) are individually documented as Sherbro/Susu foundations, so they were still removed.
   - **Mathora** (Cholifa Chiefdom, Tonkolili) — Temne *thora* "put down"; corroborates the Tonkolili Temne core.

## Phonology Check

Temne orthography/phonotactics (Mel; CV(C) syllables, no consonant clusters word-initially except in loans, frequent locative prefixes *ro-/ra-*, *ma-*, *ka-*, *ba-*, *pe-*, and frequent word-final vowels or nasals):

- **Locative prefix `ro-`**: Rokupr, Rokulan — canonical Temne (`Ro-Sar` → Lunsar shows the same prefix historically).
- **Prefix `ma-`** ("place of"): Makeni, Magburaka, Masingbi, Mapaki, Masongbon, Masiaka, Mateboi, Mambolo, Matotoka, Mahera, Mange — the single most characteristic Temne toponymic pattern.
- **Prefix `ka-`**: Kalangba, Kamaranka, Kamalo, Kambia — also canonical (cf. Temne *Ka-tem-ma*).
- **Prefix `pe-/pet-`**: Petifu, Pepel — attested Temne (*pet* "clearing/place outside the bush").
- **Others**: Lunsar, Lungi, Yele, Hunduwa, Batkanu, Gbinti, Songo, Sendugu, Foredugu, Gberi Bana, Waterloo, Port Loko.
- `Waterloo` and `Port Loko` are English/colonial-era names but are the standard, universally used names of towns inside the Temne area; they are retained as real settlement names rather than authentic Temne forms. `-dugu` in Sendugu/Foredugu is a Mande borrowing widespread in northern Sierra Leone toponymy and is well attested in Temne-majority chiefdoms.
- Prenasalised `mb`, `nd`, `ng`, `gb` (Mambolo, Hunduwa, Gbinti, Gberi Bana, Songo, Kalangba) are all legal Temne onsets. No name contains an illegal cluster.
