## What IS Confirmed About Kayan-Murik Languages
From Wikipedia Kayan-Murik languages article:
- Kayan-Murik languages: Kayan proper (Bahau, Baram, Busang, Rejang, etc.) + Murik
- Baram Kayan spoken along Baram River, including "Long Naah" and "Long Seridan" (settlements mentioned in research)
- Murik Kayan spoken on Baram River (~1,000 speakers)
- These are distinct from Kenyah languages (different branch)
