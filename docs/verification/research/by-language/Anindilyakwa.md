## Research Findings
From Anindilyakwa Land Council (anindilyakwa.com.au):
- "There are seven indigenous communities in the Groote Archipelago"
- "The Anindilyakwa people primarily reside in Angurugu and Umbakumba which are located on Groote Eylandt, and Milyakburra which is located on Bickerton Island"
- "The four additional satellite communities are: 4 Mile, Malkala, Little Paradise, and Bartalumba Bay"
- "An estimated 1500 – 2000 Anindilyakwa first language speakers live throughout the communities of the Groote Archipelago including Angurugu, Umbakumba and Milyakburra and Numbulwar"

From the same source (outstations):
- Angurugu outstations: Ananja, Emerald River, Leske Pools, Yenbakwa
- Umbakumba outstations: Thompsons Bay, Salt Lake, Six Mile
