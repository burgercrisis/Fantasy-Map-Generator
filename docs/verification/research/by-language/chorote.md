The Chorote people were traditionally nomadic hunter-gatherers in the Gran Chaco. They lived in temporary camps and semi-sedentary villages. They did not have fixed place names in the Western sense. Their territory was defined by geographic features (rivers, lakes) and seasonal camps.

### Research Findings
- The Chorote live along the Pilcomayo River in Argentina and Paraguay
- The southern branch is concentrated near Tartagal (Salta, Argentina)
- A splinter faction lives near Colonia Pedro P. Pena (Paraguay)
- The northern tribes live near the western border of Paraguay
- In Bolivia, only a few families remain in Tarija department
- The Chorote were settled in missions by Franciscans and later Mennonites
- They now live in villages of 1-100 extended families
