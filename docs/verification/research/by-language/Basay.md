## Research Findings
From Wikipedia Basay language:
- Extinct Formosan language of northern Taiwan
- Spoken around modern-day Taipei by Basay, Qauqaut, and Trobiawan peoples
- Dialects: Basay proper, Trobiawan, Linaw–Qauqaut
- Li (1992): four Basaic languages: Basay, Luilang, Nankan, Puting
- Field notes from Shinshe (Basay speaker) and Yilan (Trobiawan dialect speaker)

From Taipei Times (2025-2026):
- Kipatauw in Beitou was a Basay settlement with its own related language
- "individual settlements such as Kipatauw in Beitou had their own related languages"
- Modern Tamshui occupies the site of Basay village Senar

From Columbia University/Spanish Formosa sources:
- Taparri and Kimaurri were Basay villages in Keelung area
- Spanish founded churches in Taparri and Kimaurri (1631–1633)
- "The people of the village of Kimauri understand the languages of the other eight villages"

From blog post (iplmyths):
- Dalongdong was first settled by the Basay tribe
- Nankan is a native community in northern Taiwan

From Luzhu District Wikipedia:
- Nankan Village in Luzhu District was earliest area in Taoyuan to be developed
