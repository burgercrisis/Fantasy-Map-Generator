## Linguistic Observations

Cavineña is a Tacanan language spoken by ~600-1,200 people in the Bolivian Amazon. The Cavineño people live in 6 small communities, with Puerto Cavinas being the largest. The language has a complex phonological inventory including palatal and labiovelar consonants, and six tense/aspect markers. Most speakers are bilingual in Spanish.

- **Typical suffixes**: -na, -ña (language/people suffix)
- **Typical prefixes**: None standard
- **Common patterns**: Settlement names are mostly Spanish-derived (mission names)
- **Phonotactic notes**: CV syllable structure, no complex clusters
