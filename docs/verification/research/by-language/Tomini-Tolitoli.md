## Problem: Not a Single Language
"Tomini-Tolitoli" is a disputed subgroup containing two branches:
- **Tolitoli**: Totoli (~25,000 speakers), Boano (~2,700 speakers)
- **Tomini**: Tialo/Tomini (~30,000), Lauje (~38,000), Ampibabo (~6,000), Dondo (~13,000), Balaesang (~3,200), Pendau (~3,200), Dampelas (~10,300), Taje (~350), Tajio (~12,000)

Total ~145,000 speakers across 10+ distinct languages. Some varieties (Totoli) are lexically divergent from the Tomini branch.
