## WAITING JustIFICATION

Alu/Dia is a small language with ~1,800 speakers in Sandaun Province, PNG. After 2 separate web searches, **no specific Alu/Dia village names** are documented in publicly available sources.

The original entry contained:
- **Province/region names** (Sandaun, Sepik Coast)
- **Language family names** (Torricelli, Wapei)
- **Alternative language names** (Dia, Sinagen, Galu, Metru)
- **Other Wapei language names** (Yis, Yau, Olo, Elkei, Au, Yil, Ningil, Gnau, Yapunda, Valman)

None of these are valid Alu place names. The other Wapei languages (Au, Olo, Gnau, etc.) are distinct languages spoken in their own villages by their own communities — not Alu-speaking villages.

Per Rule 5: Small language with no documented toponymy after 2 searches. Marked WAITING.
