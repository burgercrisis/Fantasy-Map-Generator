## Research Log
- Search 1: "Mapudungun language" → Wikipedia: Language isolate (or Araucanian family) spoken in Chile and Argentina. ~410,000 speakers (2024). Spoken in south-central Chile and west-central Argentina. Also called Mapuche, Mapuzugun, Araucanian.
- Search 2: "Mapudungun language Chile" → Wikipedia/UNESCO: Spoken in south-central Chile (Araucanía, Los Ríos, Los Lagos). ~286,967 speakers in Chile. Definitely endangered. 3 dialect groups: north, central, south (8 sub-groups).
- Search 3: "Mapudungun language Argentina" → Wikipedia: Spoken in Neuquén, Río Negro, La Pampa, Chubut, Buenos Aires. ~8,400 speakers in Argentina. Neuquén/Río Negro similar to central Chile dialect; La Pampa (Ranquel) closer to northern Chile dialect.
- Search 4: "Mapudungun language Mapuche" → Wikipedia/OMNIGLOT: Also called Mapuche, Mapuzugun, Araucanian. Language isolate or Araucanian family. ~410,000 total speakers (2024). Official in Galvarino, Padre Las Casas, Temuco (Chile).
