## Critical Issues

### 1. Not a Single Language (Cover Term)
"Aboriginal Pidgin English" is NOT a single language. It's a cover term for multiple pidgin English varieties that developed independently across Australia:
- **Port Jackson Pidgin English** (Sydney/Newcastle region, 1788+)
- **Queensland Pidgin English** (developed from NSW Pidgin, spread inland)
- **Northern Territory Pidgin English** (later creolized into Kriol)
- **Western Australian Pidgin English**
- **South Australian Pidgin English**

These varieties developed in different regions with different Aboriginal language substrates. They are not one language.

### 2. All Names Are English Colonial Names
Every name in the entry is an English name given by British settlers:
- Sydney (named after Lord Sydney, British Home Secretary)
- Parramatta (from Darug language, but the English form is what's used)
- Brisbane (named after Sir Thomas Brisbane, governor)
- Adelaide (named after Queen Adelaide)
- Perth (named after Perth, Scotland)
- Melbourne (named after Lord Melbourne)

These were NOT coined by Aboriginal Pidgin English speakers. They are English names that pidgin speakers USED, but the names themselves are not "from" the pidgin language.
