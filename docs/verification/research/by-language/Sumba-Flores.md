## Problem: Conflates Two Separate Islands
"Sumba-Flores" combines two distinct islands in the Lesser Sunda chain:
- **Sumba**: Sumba languages (Kambera, Wejewa, Lamboya, Kodi, Wanukaka, Anakalangu, Mamboru)
- **Flores**: Flores languages (Ngada, Sikka, Lamaholot, Ende, Manggarai)

These islands have completely different language families. The current entry contains mostly Sumba-related names with NO Flores names despite "Flores" being in the entry name.
