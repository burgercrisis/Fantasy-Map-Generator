# Awakatek (i=75) — Re-verification

## Language
Awakatek (also known as Aguacateco, Awaketec, Coyotin, Chalchitec) — a Mayan language from the Mamean branch, spoken primarily in the municipality of Aguacatán, Huehuetenango, Guatemala. Also spoken in Campeche and Chiapas, Mexico by descendants who migrated during the Guatemalan Civil War.
