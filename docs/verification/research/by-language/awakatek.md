## Linguistic Observations
- Awakatek is a Mayan language of the Ixilan branch (closely related to Ixil)
- Spoken only in the municipality of Aguacatán, Huehuetenango (the ONLY municipality where it's native)
- Also spoken by refugee communities in Campeche, Mexico (Maya Tecún, Santo Domingo Kesté)
- Has a closely related dialect/language called Chalchitek (Chalchitán area)
- Also spoken in Chiapas, Mexico and diaspora in USA (Alabama, NC, Miami)
