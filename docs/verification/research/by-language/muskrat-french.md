# Muskrat French (Entry 20/232)

## Language Facts
- **Name**: Muskrat French (Mushrat French, Detroit River French Canadien)
- **Family**: Indo-European > Romance > French (Canadian French dialect)
- **Speakers**: ~5,000 heritage claimants; fluent dialect speakers virtually extinct
- **Regions**: Detroit River corridor: Wayne/Monroe counties (MI), Essex County (ON), western Lake Erie to Sandusky (OH)
- **Status**: Moribund; English dominant since mid-20th century
